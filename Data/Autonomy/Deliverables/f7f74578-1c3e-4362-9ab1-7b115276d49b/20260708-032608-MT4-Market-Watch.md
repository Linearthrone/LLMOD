### Setups / Plays
The system triggers alerts based on three primary "Opportunity States":

1.  **The Momentum Pivot:** MACD signal line crossover coinciding with price breaking the 20-period EMA.
    *   **Entry:** Limit order at the break-point.
    *   **Stop:** 20-30 pips (Instrument dependent).
    *   **Exit:** Take Profit at 1:2 Risk/Reward or opposite MACD crossover.
2.  **The Exhaustion Fade:** RSI reaching extremes (>70 or <30) while price touches a historical H1 support/resistance level.
    *   **Entry:** Market execution on candle close.
    *   **Stop:** Above/below the signal candle's wick.
    *   **Exit:** Mid-point of the RSI range (50).
3.  **The Trend Convergence:** Price aligned with both Fast and Slow MAs, with RSI trending in the direction of the move (not yet overextended).
    *   **Entry:** Pullback to the Fast MA.
    *   **Stop:** 20 pips.
    *   **Exit:** Trailing stop based on the Slow MA.
