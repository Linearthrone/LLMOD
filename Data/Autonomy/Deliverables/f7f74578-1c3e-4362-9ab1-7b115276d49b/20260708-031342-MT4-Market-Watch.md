## Strategy Overview
The Market Watch operates as a high-level filter, scanning a curated watchlist of 15 instruments (FX Majors, Crosses, Metals, and Indices) on the H1 timeframe. It does not execute trades itself but acts as the "eyes" of the system, identifying high-probability setups based on technical confluence.

**Instruments:** 
- FX: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY
- Metals: XAUUSD, XAGUSD
- Indices: US30, US500, NAS100

**Signal Logic:**
- **Trend Filtering:** Price relative to a 200-period SMA to ensure we are trading with the dominant tide.
- **Momentum Confirmation:** RSI (14) must be above 60 for Longs or below 40 for Shorts.
- **Crossover Trigger:** A fast EMA (9) crossing a slow EMA (21) to identify the precise entry window.
- **Volatility Guard:** ATR-based checks to ensure the market is neither dead nor dangerously erratic.

## Setups / Plays
When the Market Watch identifies a confluence of the above, it routes a "Signal Opportunity" to the execution block with the following parameters:

- **Entry:** Market execution upon H1 candle close following the EMA crossover.
- **Exit:** Dynamic trailing stop or fixed Take Profit based on a 1:2 Risk/Reward ratio.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed at the recent swing high/low or 1.5x ATR from the entry price to avoid noise-induced stop-outs.
- **Position Sizing:** Defaulted to 0.01 lots for verification, scalable to 1% of equity once the "Green Light" for live capital is granted.
