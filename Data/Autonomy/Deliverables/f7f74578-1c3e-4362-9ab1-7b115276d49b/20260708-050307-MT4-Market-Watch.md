## Strategy Overview
The Market Watch is not a single strategy, but a high-frequency monitoring layer designed to identify high-probability setups across 15 core instruments (including XAUUSD, NAS100, and EURUSD) on the H1 timeframe.

**Core Rules:**
1. **Trend Filter**: Price must be above/below the 200-period SMA to determine primary bias.
2. **Momentum Signal**: RSI (14) must be crossing out of overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) territories.
3. **Confirmation**: MACD (12, 26, 9) histogram must show a change in polarity (positive to negative or vice versa) in alignment with the RSI.
4. **Routing**: Valid signals are automatically routed to the backtest block for validation before being presented as a trade candidate.

## Setups / Plays
The system focuses on **Mean Reversion** and **Trend Following** breakouts.

- **Entry**: Long when Price > 200 SMA, RSI < 30 (oversold dip in uptrend), and MACD histogram turns positive.
- **Exit**: Take Profit (TP) at the next major liquidity zone or a fixed 40-pip target.
- **Stop Loss (SL)**: Hard stop at 20 pips or the most recent swing low/high.
- **Position Sizing**: Strictly 0.01 lots for demo validation; scaled based on the account equity for live execution.
