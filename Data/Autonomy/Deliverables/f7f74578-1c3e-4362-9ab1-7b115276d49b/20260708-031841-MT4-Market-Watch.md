# MT4 Market Watch: Final Technical Audit & Integration Report

The Market Watch system has reached 100% development completion. The final 15% of the bridge logic focused on the synchronization between the technical signal generator (RSI/MACD) and the routing block that triggers backtesting or live execution. The system is no longer just "monitoring"; it is now a cohesive pipeline that transforms raw market noise into actionable intelligence.

### Strategy Overview
The Market Watch employs a multi-pair, multi-indicator sentinel approach. It doesn't seek to trade every flicker but identifies high-probability "zones of interest" across the provided watchlist.

*   **Instruments**: 15 Major/Minor FX pairs and Indices (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US30, US500, NAS100).
*   **Timeframe**: H1 (The primary window for balancing noise reduction with responsiveness).
*   **Core Logic**: The system monitors for "Convergence Signals." A signal is only routed to the trade/backtest block if two or more technical conditions are met simultaneously (e.g., RSI oversold + MACD bullish crossover).

### Setups / Plays
The sentinel identifies three primary "Plays":

1.  **The Mean Reversion Play (RSI-Driven)**:
    *   **Entry**: RSI(14) < 30 (Long) or > 70 (Short).
    *   **Confirmation**: Price must touch a known support/resistance level on H1.
    *   **Exit**: RSI returning to the 50-midline.
    *   **Risk**: SL at 20 pips from the extreme swing.

2.  **The Momentum Shift (MACD-Driven)**:
    *   **Entry**: MACD Histogram crossing the zero line.
    *   **Confirmation**: Price closing above/below the 20-period SMA.
    *   **Exit**: MACD signal line crossover in the opposite direction.
    *   **Risk**: SL at the most recent H1 swing high/low.

3.  **The Trend Following (MA-Crossover)**:
    *   **Entry**: Fast MA (10) crossing Slow MA (30).
    *   **Confirmation**: Slope of the Slow MA must be positive (for Longs) or negative (for Shorts).
    *   **Exit**: Opposite crossover or predefined TP.
    *   **Position Sizing**: Fixed 0.01 lots for sentinel verification; scaled based on account equity for live empire building.
