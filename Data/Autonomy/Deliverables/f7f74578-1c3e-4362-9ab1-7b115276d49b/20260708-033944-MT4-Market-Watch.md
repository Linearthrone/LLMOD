### Strategy Overview
The Market Watch system is designed as a high-sensitivity filtration layer. It does not execute trades autonomously but instead identifies high-probability setups across a diversified basket of 15 instruments (FX Majors, Metals, and Indices) on the H1 timeframe.

**Instruments:** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US30, US500, NAS100.
**Timeframe:** H1 (1-Hour).
**Logic:** The system monitors a composite of three primary technical indicators:
1. **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** Identifying extreme overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) conditions.
2. **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Tracking momentum shifts and signal line crossovers.
3. **MA (Moving Average):** Price relation to the 50-period and 200-period SMA for trend confirmation.

### Setups / Plays
The sentinel triggers an alert when a "Confluence Event" occurs:
- **Bullish Play:** Price is above 200 SMA + RSI < 30 (oversold dip in uptrend) + MACD Histogram flipping positive.
- **Bearish Play:** Price is below 200 SMA + RSI > 70 (overbought rally in downtrend) + MACD Histogram flipping negative.
- **Exit/Stop:** The system suggests a hard Stop Loss (SL) of 20-30 pips based on recent ATR (Average True Range), with a target Take Profit (TP) of 40-60 pips (1:2 Risk/Reward).
