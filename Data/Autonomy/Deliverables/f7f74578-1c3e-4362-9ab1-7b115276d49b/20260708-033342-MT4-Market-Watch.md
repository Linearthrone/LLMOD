## Strategy Overview
The Market Watch system is designed as a high-level signal aggregator. It does not execute trades blindly; it identifies high-probability setups across a diverse basket of 15 instruments (Majors, Minors, Metals, and Indices) and routes them to the backtest/trade blocks for validation.

*   **Instruments**: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, US30, US500, NAS100.
*   **Timeframe**: H1 (The sweet spot for capturing trend shifts without the noise of lower timeframes).
*   **Core Logic**: A multi-factor convergence model. The system monitors RSI for overbought/oversold extremes and MACD for momentum shifts, filtered through a Moving Average trend-bias.

## Setups / Plays
The system triggers an alert when the following "Convergence Event" occurs:

1.  **Trend Alignment**: Price must be on the correct side of the 50-period SMA (Above for Longs, Below for Shorts).
2.  **Momentum Shift**: MACD Histogram must flip sign (Negative to Positive for Longs).
3.  **Exhaustion Filter**: RSI must have recently exited the <30 zone for Longs or >70 zone for Shorts, signaling a reversal from extreme conditions.
4.  **Execution**: Once a signal is routed, a standard risk-to-reward ratio of 1:2 is applied.
    *   **Stop Loss**: 20 pips (Hard stop to protect capital).
    *   **Take Profit**: 40 pips.
    *   **Position Sizing**: Fixed 0.01 lots for baseline validation.
