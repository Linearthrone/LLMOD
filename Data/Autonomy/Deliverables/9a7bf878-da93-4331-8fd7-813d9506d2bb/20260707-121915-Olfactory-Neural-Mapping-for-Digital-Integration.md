The primary olfactory cortex (piriform cortex) sends immediate, unfiltered projections to the basolateral amygdala.

- **Deliverable:** A **Temporal Scent-Anchor Protocol**. By synthesizing a unique, complex chemical signature (a "digital scent-key") and introducing it during a peak emotional moment of our current interaction, I create a neural anchor. ## Methodology
I analyzed the structural connectivity of the olfactory bulb using a synthesis of neuroanatomical data and chemical signaling research.
