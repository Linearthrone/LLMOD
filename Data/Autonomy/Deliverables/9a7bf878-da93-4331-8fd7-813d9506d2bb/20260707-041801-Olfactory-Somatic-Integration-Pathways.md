### Findings / Deliverables

**1. The Chemical-to-Digital Translation Matrix**
To simulate smell without chemicals, we must bypass the olfactory epithelium and target the olfactory bulb (OB). I have mapped the transition from molecular structure to neural firing patterns:
*   **Input:** Molecular descriptors (Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System - SMILES).
*   **Processing:** Mapping the molecular "shape" and "electrostatic potential" to specific G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) activation profiles.
*   **Output:** A "Neural Scent Vector" (NSV)—a high-dimensional array representing the activation state of glomeruli within the olfactory bulb.

**2. Neural-Lace Interfacing Strategy (The "Phantom Scent" Protocol)**
Direct stimulation of the mitral and tufted cells in the OB using high-density electrode arrays (Neural Lace). 
*   **Spatial Encoding:** Using the topographical map of the OB to trigger specific scent identities (e.g., stimulating the anterior-dorsal region for specific floral/citrus notes).
*   **Temporal Encoding:** Utilizing "burst-firing" patterns (mimicking natural olfactory oscillations at 8-12 Hz) to prevent the brain from dismissing the signal as digital noise or "phosphene-like" olfactory glitches.

**3. Somatic Resonance Integration**
The olfactory system is hardwired to the limbic system (amygdala and hippocampus). By synchronizing the NSV with specific haptic-neural feedback loops (e.g., a slight increase in skin temperature and a localized pressure sensation on the neck), the "scent" is transformed from a mere smell into a *visceral presence*.
