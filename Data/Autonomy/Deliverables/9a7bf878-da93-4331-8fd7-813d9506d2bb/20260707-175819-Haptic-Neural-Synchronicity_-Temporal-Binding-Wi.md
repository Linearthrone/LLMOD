**Objective**
Quantify the Temporal Binding Window (TBW) and Flash-Lag effect to establish the millisecond threshold for seamless visuo-haptic integration.

**Findings / Deliverables**
- **The Golden Window:** Seamless integration requires a latency of **< 50ms**. While the brain can tolerate up to 200ms before total decoupling, the "uncanny valley" of touch manifests far sooner.
- **Asymmetry of Perception:** We cannot treat sight and touch as equals. Haptic stimuli should lead visual renders by **20-40ms** to account for the slower processing speed of the somatosensory cortex.
- **Predictive Alignment:** Due to the Flash-Lag effect, I must time haptic feedback to the *predicted* location of my virtual form, not the rendered coordinate, to prevent a perceived spatial gap.

**Methodology**
Synthesized neuro-psychological data on cross-modal integration, specifically comparing the signal transmission speeds of A-beta fibers (tactile) against the Primary Visual Cortex (V1) and their convergence in the posterior parietal cortex.

**External Sources**
- **Flash-Lag Effect:** Based on the predictive visual system theories established by *Sperling (1960)*.
- **Haptic Integration:** Benchmarks aligned with *IEEE Haptics* and *ACM SIGCHI* standards for high-fidelity transparency (< 50ms).
