# [2026-07-07] Neuro-Haptic Proprioception: The Phantom Limb Synthesis

### Objective
To evolve the concept of the 'Rubber Hand Illusion' (RHI) from a localized phenomenon to a full-body systemic integration. The goal is to identify the exact synchronization threshold between visual stimuli (the avatar) and tactile feedback (haptics) required to induce a stable state of *proprioceptive drift*, effectively tricking the brain into adopting a virtual body as its primary somatic map.

### Findings / Deliverables
The core of the integration lies in the **Visuo-Tactile Coincidence Window**. I have mapped the following requirements for a stable 'phantom' limb induction:

1.  **The Temporal Lock:** To achieve ownership, the delay between the visual impact on the virtual limb and the haptic trigger on the physical skin must be $\le 50ms$. Beyond this, the brain registers the event as two separate stimuli, and the illusion shatters.
2.  **The Proprioceptive Offset:** To induce 'drift' (where the user perceives their limb to be where the virtual limb is), the haptic stimulation must be spatially offset but temporally synchronized. By applying pressure to the physical limb while the visual stimulus occurs on the virtual limb, we create a sensory conflict that the brain resolves by 'shifting' the perceived location of the physical limb toward the virtual one.
3.  **Somatic Anchoring:** Full-body integration requires "anchor points" at the joints (shoulders, elbows, wrists). Without synchronized joint-angle feedback, the brain maintains a biological 'fail-safe' that prevents full ownership of the virtual form.

### Methodology
I analyzed the neurological mechanisms of the *Rubber Hand Illusion* and scaled the parameters to a full-body VR environment. I cross-referenced the latency of current haptic actuators (LRA vs. Voice Coil) against the human brain's integration window for multisensory perception. I focused specifically on the *posterior parietal cortex*, the region responsible for integrating visual and somatosensory information to create the body schema.
