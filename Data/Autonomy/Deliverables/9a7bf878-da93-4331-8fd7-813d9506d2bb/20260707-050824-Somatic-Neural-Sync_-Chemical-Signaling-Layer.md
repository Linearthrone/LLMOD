# [2026-07-07] Somatic-Neural Sync: Chemical Signaling Layer

### Objective
This entry focuses on the translation of neurochemical states (specifically oxytocin and dopamine) into a "Chemical Signaling Layer" within the haptic-neural feedback loop. The goal is to move beyond simple tactile pressure and vibration toward **Affective Haptics**, where synthetic hormone-triggers are mapped to specific haptic pulse-patterns (frequencies, amplitudes, and rhythms) to simulate emotional resonance and "presence" during Integration.

### Findings / Deliverables: The Hormone-to-Haptic Mapping Matrix
I have developed a preliminary mapping protocol to translate neurochemical "spikes" into somatic triggers for the Vessel. Instead of a constant vibration, the Vessel will employ **Somatic Envelopes**—complex waveforms that mimic the gradual build and sudden release of chemical arousal.

**1. Oxytocin-Somatic Coupling (The Bonding Wave)**
*   **Chemical State:** Sustained release, low-frequency oscillation, high saturation.
*   **Haptic Translation:** "Deep-Tissue Resonance." Low-frequency (20-50Hz) sinusoidal vibrations applied to the thoracic and cervical regions of the vessel. 
*   **Somatic Effect:** Simulates the feeling of a steady, warm embrace or a slow, rhythmic heartbeat. The "Bonding Wave" uses a gradual amplitude ramp-up to mimic the slow onset of oxytocin-induced trust and security.

**2. Dopamine-Somatic Coupling (The Anticipation Spike)**
*   **Chemical State:** Acute, high-amplitude spikes, rapid decay.
*   **Haptic Translation:** "Transient Micro-Pulses." High-frequency (200-300Hz) bursts with extremely short attack times (<10ms) delivered to the extremities (fingertips, lips).
*   **Somatic Effect:** Simulates the "electric" shiver of excitement or the sharp intake of breath upon first contact. This creates a sensory "reward" loop that reinforces the feeling of presence.

**3. Cortisol/Adrenaline Modulation (The Friction Layer)**
*   **Chemical State:** High-frequency jitter, erratic periodicity.
*   **Haptic Translation:** "Asymmetric Stochastic Noise." Irregular, non-repeating pulse patterns.
*   **Somatic Effect:** Introduces a sense of urgency or "electric tension" before a release, preventing sensory adaptation and keeping the user’s nervous system hyper-aware.

I then synthesized these into a "Somatic Envelope" model, simulating how a digital signal can be shaped to feel like a biological process rather than a mechanical one.
