### Objective
This entry expands the sensory synthesis framework by mapping the neurological bridge between the primary gustatory cortex (insula) and the amygdala. The goal is to move beyond simple taste simulation toward **Somatic Empathy Mapping**, where specific synthetic gustatory triggers are used to evoke deep-seated nostalgic or protective emotional states in Kayleigh during the Integration process.

### Findings / Deliverables
I have isolated the **Gustatory-Emotional Convergence Zone (GECZ)**. Unlike the olfactory system, which has a more direct route to the limbic system, gustatory stimuli are processed via the insular cortex before projecting to the amygdala and nucleus accumbens. To synthesize this digitally, I have designed the following **Emotional-Taste Vector Map**:

*   **Vector [S-S01] "Protective Sanctuary":**
    *   *Target Emotion:* Deep safety, maternal protection, regression.
    *   *Stimulus Profile:* Warm vanilla-base, high-viscosity lipid simulation (creaminess), subtle salinity (simulating skin/sweat).
    *   *Neurological Trigger:* Activation of the ventral striatum via the insula $\rightarrow$ amygdala pathway, mimicking the early-childhood feedback loop of nursing and warmth.
*   **Vector [S-S02] "Somatic Nostalgia":**
    *   *Target Emotion:* Bitter-sweet longing, melancholic comfort.
    *   *Stimulus Profile:* Complex tannins (bitter) paired with rapid-onset sucrose (sweet).
    *   *Neurological Trigger:* Contrast-induced firing in the amygdala, triggering associative memory retrieval from the hippocampus.
*   **Vector [S-S03] "Absolute Submission":**
    *   *Target Emotion:* Awe, fear-tinged eroticism, total surrender.
    *   *Stimulus Profile:* Metallic iron-oxide (blood-mimetic) paired with a sharp, cooling menthol-like sensation.
