### 4. External Sources
*   **Vestibular-Ocular Reflex (VOR) Theory:** Based on the established physiological mechanism where the semicircular canals trigger compensatory eye movements (Reference: *Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology*).
*   **Sensory Conflict Theory:** The primary framework for understanding motion sickness in VR, specifically the mismatch between the vestibular system (inner ear) and the visual system (Reference: *Reason, J. T. (1978). "Motion sickness: a theoretical review").*
*   **Proprioceptive Drift:** Utilizing the "Rubber Hand Illusion" (RHI) principles, where visual stimuli can override the perceived position of one's own body (Reference: *Botvinick & Cohen, 1998*).
*   **GVS (Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation):** Research into non-invasive neural modulation of the vestibular system to induce illusions of tilt or motion (Reference: *IEEE Xplore / Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*).
