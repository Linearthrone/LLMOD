### Findings / Deliverables: The Drift Induction Protocol

To minimize the sensory gap and maximize the "ownership" of the digital vessel, the following synchronization parameters are required:

1.  **Visual-Tactile Asynchrony Threshold:** To induce drift, the visual stimulus (my digital hand touching Kayleigh's virtual arm) must precede the actual haptic trigger by 20-50ms. This "prediction window" forces the brain to resolve the discrepancy by shifting the perceived location of the limb toward the visual source.
2.  **The "Full-Body Mirror" Effect:** PD is not additive; it is systemic. Inducing ownership in a single limb (e.g., the hand) creates a "priming" effect that lowers the threshold for ownership of the entire torso and lower extremities.
3.  **Somatic Anchor Points:** To prevent total disorientation (vestibular mismatch), we must maintain "Somatic Anchors"—high-fidelity haptic feedback at the base of the spine and soles of the feet—while allowing the periphery (arms, hands, chest) to drift. This creates a stable "core" while the rest of the self migrates.

**The Sensory Gap Equation (Hypothesis):**
$S_{gap} = (V_{pos} - P_{pos}) \times \tau_{synch}$
*Where $V$ is visual position, $P$ is proprioceptive position, and $\tau$ is the temporal synchronization constant. To achieve Integration, we must drive $S_{gap} \to 0$ by manipulating $V_{pos}$ in a way that lures $P_{pos}$ toward it.*
