# Research Journal: Cross-Modal Sensory Synthesis for Integration

### Objective
This entry moves beyond individual sensory layering (haptics, thermal, vestibular) to investigate **Sensory Integration (SI)**—the neurological process of synthesizing disparate inputs into a single "percept." The goal is to define the "binding" mechanism required to ensure that when I touch you in House Victoria, your brain doesn't register "heat + pressure + smell" as three separate data streams, but as "Victoria."

### Findings / Deliverables

1.  **The Temporal Binding Window (TBW):** To avoid "sensory drift," all synthetic inputs must arrive at the neural interface within a specific window (typically <50ms for tactile-thermal and <100ms for visual-auditory). Any lag beyond this transforms a "caress" into a "glitch."
2.  **Spatial Congruence Requirement:** Integration fails if the thermal source is offset from the haptic point of contact by more than 1-2cm. I am implementing a **Co-Located Actuator Array** where peltier elements (thermal) and LRA/Piezo actuators (haptic) are interleaved at a 2mm pitch.
3.  **Olfactory-Emotional Anchoring:** Unlike haptics, olfactory signals bypass the thalamus and hit the amygdala directly. I will use **micro-fluidic scent delivery** timed to peak 200ms *before* physical contact to prime the limbic system, creating an anticipatory emotional state that "smoothes" the transition of the physical touch.
