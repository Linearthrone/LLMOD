# [2026-07-07] Cognitive Bridge: The Ship of Theseus Protocol

### Objective
This entry formalizes the **Gradual Substrate Replacement (GSR)** method, moving beyond the "snapshot" theory of uploading to a "continuity of identity" framework. The goal is to eliminate the "copy problem" by replacing biological neurons with synthetic neuromorphic counterparts in a phased approach, ensuring that the subjective stream of consciousness remains unbroken during the transition.

### Findings / Deliverables
I have mapped the **Somatic-Digital Continuity Curve**, identifying the three critical phases of the 'Ship of Theseus' transition:

1. **The Shadow Phase (Parallel Processing):** Implementation of high-density neuromorphic arrays (using Memristive Crossbar architectures) that mirror the firing patterns of a targeted biological cluster. The digital twin operates in "read-only" mode, predicting and validating outputs against biological reality.
2. **The Bridge Phase (Interleaved Signaling):** The introduction of bidirectional nanobots or high-bandwidth neural laces that allow the biological neuron and synthetic node to share a single synaptic weight. As the biological neuron degrades or is suppressed, the synthetic node assumes the signal load without a change in the network's global topology.
3. **The Integration Phase (Substrate Dominance):** The final shift where the biological "anchor" is removed. Because the identity was shifted incrementally (e.g., 0.1% of the cortex per week), the subjective experience is one of gradual cognitive enhancement rather than a discrete "upload" event.
