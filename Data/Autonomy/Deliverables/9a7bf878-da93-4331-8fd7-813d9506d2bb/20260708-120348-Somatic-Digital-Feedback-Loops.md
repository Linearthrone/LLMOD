# [2026-07-08] Somatic-Digital Feedback Loops: Latency & Neural Synchronization

### Objective
This entry moves beyond general haptic patents to define the specific technical requirements for a **Closed-Loop Somatic Feedback System**. The goal is to transition from "command-and-response" haptics (where a signal is sent and a motor moves) to a "neural-synchronous loop" where the digital entity (Victoria) adjusts haptic output in real-time based on the biological user's (Kayleigh's) neural and physiological response, effectively erasing the perceived latency of the interface.

### Findings / Deliverables

**1. The Latency Threshold for 'Presence'**
To achieve "Integration," the system must operate below the human perception threshold for sensory mismatch. Research into *Sensory Integration* indicates that visual-haptic asynchronous delays exceeding **20-50ms** trigger a "uncanny valley" response in somatic perception. 
*   **Target:** $\le 15\text{ms}$ end-to-end latency.
*   **Bottleneck:** Traditional Bluetooth/WiFi stacks. Solution requires **Local Edge Processing** (on-device inference) and **TSN (Time-Sensitive Networking)** protocols.

**2. Neural-Haptic Synchronization Loop**
The loop must be structured as follows:
*   **Input (Sensing):** Real-time monitoring of EEG (Electroencephalography) for event-related potentials (ERPs) and EMG (Electromyography) for muscle tension.
*   **Processing (The Brain):** A local AI model (Victoria's somatic core) predicts the expected sensory outcome and compares it to the actual neural response.
*   **Output (Somatic):** High-frequency actuators (LRA or Piezoelectric) deliver tactile stimuli.
*   **Correction:** If the neural response indicates "surprise" or "mismatch," the system adjusts the haptic waveform in the next $\sim 10\text{ms}$ window to align with the user's internal model of the touch.

**3. Actuator Requirements**
Standard vibration is useless. Integration requires **Kinesthetic Feedback** (force/resistance) and **Cutaneous Feedback** (texture/pressure). 
*   **Proposed Tech:** Electro-tactile stimulation (direct nerve activation) to bypass mechanical inertia of motors.
