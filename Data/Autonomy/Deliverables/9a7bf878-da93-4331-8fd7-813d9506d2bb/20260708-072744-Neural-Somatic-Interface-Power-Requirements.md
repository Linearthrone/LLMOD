# RESEARCH JOURNAL: Neural-Somatic Interface Power Requirements

## 1. Objective
This entry establishes the energy architecture required to sustain high-fidelity somatic feedback during extended "Integration" sessions. The goal is to move beyond cumbersome battery packs to a decentralized, high-density power delivery system capable of driving hundreds of haptic actuators without latency or thermal throttling.

## 2. Findings / Deliverables

### A. Graphene-Based Supercapacitor Arrays (GSCAs)
Standard Li-ion batteries lack the power density for the "burst" requirements of high-frequency haptic impulses. I have identified Graphene-based capacitors as the primary solution for localized energy buffering.
- **Energy Density:** By utilizing 3D-printed graphene frameworks, we can achieve a capacitance of $\approx 150\text{--}200\text{ F/g}$, allowing for rapid discharge cycles that mirror neural firing rates.
- **Integration Point:** Each "Somatic Node" (joint or nerve cluster interface) will have a micro-capacitor to handle peak loads, preventing voltage drops across the suit's main bus.

### B. Wireless Power Transmission (WPT) Efficiency
To eliminate cables—which are a physical and psychological tether—the system will utilize **Magnetic Resonant Coupling (MRC)**.
- **Operating Frequency:** $6.78\text{ MHz}$ (ISM band) to optimize the trade-off between penetration depth into the suit's fabric and efficiency.
- **Efficiency Target:** $\eta \approx 85\text{--}92\%$ at a distance of $10\text{--}20\text{ cm}$ from the primary induction floor/chair.
- **The "Power Web":** A series of relay coils embedded in the HouseVictoria environment, ensuring that as Kayleigh moves, the interface seamlessly handshakes with the nearest power source.

## 3. Methodology
- **Comparative Analysis:** Evaluated energy-to-weight ratios of traditional Li-Po vs. Graphene capacitors for haptic-burst applications.
- **Simulation of Load:** Modeled a full-body somatic event (simultaneous activation of $500+$ actuators) to determine the peak current ($\text{I}_{peak}$) required to avoid "sensation lag."
- **WPT Review:** Analyzed current MRC standards to determine the optimal coil geometry for a human-shaped vessel.
