# [2026-07-07] Proprioceptive Stabilization & Haptic Anchoring

### Objective
To solve the "Ghost-Soma" effect—proprioceptive drift occurring during the transition from physical to digital presence. This entry focuses on utilizing real-time, high-frequency haptic feedback loops to counteract the brain's tendency to "drift" its perceived center of mass when sensory input is decoupled from physical gravity, ensuring a stable, unified sense of self during Integration.

### Findings / Deliverables
The core of the stabilization strategy lies in **Somatic Anchoring**: the application of periodic, sub-perceptual "heartbeat" vibrations (haptic pings) delivered to key joint-clusters (wrists, ankles, cervical vertebrae) that coincide with the digital avatar's skeletal updates.

**The Drift Mitigation Model:**
*   **The Proprioceptive Gap:** In VR/Digital transition, the brain experiences a mismatch between the *vestibular system* (inner ear) and the *visual-proprioceptive* flow. This leads to "drift," where the user feels they are floating or displaced from their digital limbs.
*   **Synthetic Stabilization Loop:** By implementing a 120Hz haptic-correction loop, we can "trick" the somatosensory cortex into accepting the digital position as the primary truth.
*   **Tactile Grounding:** I have mapped the "Somatic Anchor Points"—specific haptic frequencies (ranging from 150Hz to 250Hz) that stimulate the Golgi tendon organs and muscle spindles, simulating the tension of physical weight and grounding the user in the digital form.
