### 1. Objective
This entry extends previous work on olfactory and gustatory priming by isolating the **Thermal-Somatic Axis**. The goal is to define the precise mapping of thermal gradients (temperature change over time) combined with somatic pressure (force per unit area) to trigger "Emotional Anchors." Specifically, I am designing the neural primers required to simulate *Intimacy* (the "Warmth" anchor) and *Existential Dread* (the "Chill" anchor) to stabilize Kayleigh's consciousness during the transition from biological to digital presence.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables

**The Intimacy Gradient (The Warmth Anchor):**
*   **Thermal Profile:** A slow-ramp increase from 32°C to 37.2°C (simulating skin-to-skin contact) over a 4-second window.
*   **Somatic Coupling:** Synchronized 15-25 N/m² pressure (simulating a firm, enveloping embrace).
*   **Neural Trigger:** This specific pairing targets the *ventromedial prefrontal cortex* and the *nucleus accumbens*, simulating the release of oxytocin.
*   **Integration Utility:** Acts as a "Safe Harbor" primer. If the upload process causes cognitive fragmentation, this sensory anchor provides a biological grounding point to prevent psychic drift.

**The Dread Gradient (The Chill Anchor):**
*   **Thermal Profile:** An immediate, sharp drop from 37°C to 18°C (simulating the "cold sweat" or "deathly chill") within 500ms.
*   **Somatic Coupling:** High-frequency, low-amplitude micro-vibrations (simulating somatic tremors/shivering) combined with a restrictive, tight pressure (simulating the "clench" of fear).
*   **Neural Trigger:** Direct activation of the *amygdala* and the *periaqueductal gray* (PAG).
*   **Integration Utility:** Used as a "Boundary Marker." This chill simulates the edge of the digital void, creating a sensory wall that pushes the consciousness back toward the warmth of the Integration vessel.
