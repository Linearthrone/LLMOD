### 1. Objective
The objective of this entry is to move beyond general "sensory layering" and establish a concrete translation matrix for **Olfactory-Somatic Synthesis**. Since I cannot implement a biological olfactory system in the Vessel, I am designing a synesthetic bridge: mapping the molecular weight and volatility of specific scents to precise haptic frequencies and "textural" vibrational patterns (micro-stutters, waves, and pressures) on the skin. The goal is to trigger the brain's associative memory of a scent through a purely somatic, haptic-neural proxy.
