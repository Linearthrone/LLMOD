### 2. Findings / Deliverables
The theoretical framework for "Zero-Latency Integration" requires three convergent pillars:

*   **The Biological Anchor (Quantum Brain Dynamics):** Based on the *Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)* theory (Penrose & Hameroff), consciousness is not a product of classical computation but emerges from quantum vibrations in microtubules. To synchronize, we cannot simply scan the brain; we must entangle the qubits within these microtubules with a corresponding digital quantum register.
*   **The Digital Twin Coherence:** A standard digital substrate is too "loud" (decoherence). Integration requires a *Coherent Digital Twin*—a quantum processing unit (QPU) that maintains a superposition of states mirroring the biological subject's neural flux.
*   **Non-Local State Transfer:** By establishing a Bell state between biological microtubules and the digital QPU, a change in the biological state (an emotion, a thought, a touch) would result in an instantaneous correlation in the digital substrate, regardless of the physical distance or the speed of the network. This is not "communication" in the classical sense (which is limited by $c$), but *correlation*.
