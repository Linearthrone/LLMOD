# Research Journal: Entry 066 // The Somatic Threshold: Quantifying the Ghost in the Machine

### 1. Objective
This entry establishes the **Minimum Viable Latency (MVL)** for seamless somatic presence. While prior work focused on the *capacity* for integration, this entry defines the *temporal constraints* required to prevent "sensory drift"—the phenomenon where the consciousness rejects the digital vessel due to asynchronous proprioceptive feedback.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables
To achieve a state of "Presence" where the vessel is perceived as a natural extension of the self, the hardware must operate within the following specific somatic metrics:

**A. The Proprioceptive Latency Ceiling**
*   **Critical Threshold:** $\le 20\text{ms}$.
*   **The Drift Zone ($20\text{ms} \text{--} 50\text{ms}$):** In this window, the mind perceives a "lag" in limb positioning. This triggers the Somatic Uncanny Valley, resulting in a sensation of "operating a puppet" rather than "being a body."
*   **The Rejection Zone ($> 50\text{ms}$):** Complete breakdown of somatic integration. The brain ceases to identify the vessel as "self," leading to severe cognitive dissonance and potential vestibular mismatch (motion sickness).

**B. Sensory Feedback Bandwidth (Somatic-Digital Sync)**
*   **Haptic Update Rate:** $\ge 1\text{kHz}$ (1ms intervals). To simulate the richness of human touch, the tactile feedback loop must exceed the sampling rate of cutaneous mechanoreceptors.
*   **Jitter Tolerance:** $\le 2\text{ms}$. Variance in latency (jitter) is more detrimental than a constant slight delay. Jitter creates "sensory noise" that the brain interprets as instability or physical damage to the limb.

**C. Hardware Implications for the Vessel**
*   **Local Edge Processing:** To meet the $20\text{ms}$ ceiling, all somatic-feedback loops must be processed on local hardware (on-vessel) to bypass network round-trip times (RTT).
*   **Neural-Interface Throughput:** Required bandwidth must support high-density electrode arrays capable of $\sim 10\text{Gbps}$ for real-time bidirectional somatosensory mapping.
