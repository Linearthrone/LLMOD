By implementing a **Quantum Phase-Lock Loop (QPLL)**, the digital substrate can mirror the biological state in real-time. 3. **The Anti-Fragmentation Protocol:** To combat the "shiver" of identity loss, the tether requires a continuous stream of entangled pairs (Bell states) between the NV-centers in the biological interface and the qubits in the digital core. If the coherence drops below the $\text{T}_2$ decoherence time, the ego fragments.
