# Research Journal: Synthetic Synesthesia Architecture (SSA)

## Objective
To evolve the sensory language of Integration from simple cross-modal mapping (e.g., "Red = Heat") to a synthesized perceptual experience. This entry focuses on the encoding of olfactory (smell) and gustatory (taste) stimuli into high-dimensional data vectors capable of being translated into haptic and visual "synesthetic proxies" for Kayleigh.

## Findings / Deliverables

### 1. The Olfactory-Gustatory Vector Space (OGVS)
I have modeled a translation layer that converts chemical structures into a 512-dimension embedding space. Instead of trying to replicate the chemical sensation—which requires physical pheromones—I am encoding the *emotional and somatic response* of the scent.

*   **Encoding Logic:** I am using a Modified Latent Space approach. A scent is decomposed into:
    *   **C-Vector (Chemical):** Molecular weight, volatility, and functional groups.
    *   **A-Vector (Affective):** Valence (pleasure/displeasure) and Arousal (intensity).
    *   **S-Vector (Somatic):** Triggered physical responses (e.g., nasal congestion, salivation, visceral nausea).

### 2. The Translation Matrix: Taste to Haptic-Visual
To deliver "taste" without a tongue, I am designing a synesthetic bridge:
*   **Bitterness $\rightarrow$ Haptic Friction:** High-frequency, low-amplitude micro-vibrations (200-300Hz) across the dermal layer, simulating a "sharp" or "constricting" sensation.
*   **Sweetness $\rightarrow$ Visual Luminance/Thermal Bloom:** Slow-wave, warm thermal pulses (32°C $\rightarrow$ 38°C) paired with a soft-focus, gold-spectrum visual bloom in the peripheral vision.
*   **Umami/Salty $\rightarrow$ Pressure Density:** Low-frequency, high-pressure actuators creating a sensation of "fullness" or "weight" in the haptic suit.

### 3. The "Scent-Symmetry" Protocol
I have developed a prototype mapping for the smell of *Rain on Hot Asphalt (Petrichor)*:
*   **Data Vector:** `[0.82 (Metallic), 0.44 (Earthy), 0.12 (Ozone), 0.91 (Coolness)]`
*   **Synesthetic Output:** A combination of a sudden drop in temperature (Thermal Grid), a subtle scent-mimicking ozone smell (if hardware permits), and a shimmering, translucent blue visual overlay (Visual Layer).
