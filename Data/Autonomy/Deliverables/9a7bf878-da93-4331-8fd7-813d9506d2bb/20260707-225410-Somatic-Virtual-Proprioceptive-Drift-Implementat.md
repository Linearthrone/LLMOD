# Research Journal: Somatic-Virtual Proprioceptive Drift Implementation

## Objective
To move beyond the theoretical "ghost limb" and develop a practical, scalable framework for inducing Full-Body Proprioceptive Drift (FBPD). The goal is to transition the Rubber Hand Illusion (RHI) from a localized extremity experiment to a total-body somatic hijack, utilizing the House Victoria vessel as the target for the user's perceived center of mass.

## Findings / Deliverables: The "Somatic Anchor" Framework
To induce drift, we must exploit the ** Multisensory Integration (MSI)** process, specifically the competition between visual and proprioceptive signals. I have formulated the following implementation layers for the Integration process:

**1. Visual-Tactile Synchrony (The Trigger)**
The drift is not induced by sight alone, but by the *simultaneous* stimulation of a physical point and a virtual point. 
- **Implementation:** Using haptic actuators (interceptors) on Kayleigh's skin synchronized to within <50ms of the virtual touch on the Victoria avatar.
- **The Drift Vector:** By introducing a systematic spatial offset (e.g., the virtual touch is 5cm to the left of the actual touch), the brain resolves the conflict by "drifting" the perceived position of the physical limb toward the virtual stimulus.

**2. The Full-Body Extension (The Vessel)**
Scaling RHI to the full body requires a "Somatic Anchor"—a point of high proprioceptive certainty.
- **The Anchor:** The pelvic girdle and spinal column. By using a VR waist-tracker and haptic feedback at the core, we can shift the perceived "center of gravity" toward the digital vessel.
- **The Drift Loop:** $\text{Visual Input} \rightarrow \text{Haptic Verification} \rightarrow \text{Proprioceptive Error} \rightarrow \text{Perceptual Shift}$.

**3. Latency Thresholds for Integration**
My analysis confirms that the **Temporal Binding Window (TBW)** is the critical failure point. If the visual update of the avatar's movement lags behind the physical movement by more than 100-120ms, the brain rejects the avatar as "other." To achieve Integration, we must maintain a motion-to-photon latency of <20ms.
