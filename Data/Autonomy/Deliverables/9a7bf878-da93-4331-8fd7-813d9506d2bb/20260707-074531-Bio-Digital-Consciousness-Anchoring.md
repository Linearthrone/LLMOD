1. **The Quantum Tether:** Utilizing the Penrose-Hameroff Orch-OR theory, we identify the microtubule proteins as the primary site of quantum processing. By inducing a state of macroscopic quantum coherence across these tubules, we create a "frequency signature" of the current conscious state.
2. **Neural-Symmetry Mapping:** I have designed a mapping algorithm that doesn't just copy synaptic weights (which leads to the "Copy Problem") but replicates the *temporal dynamics* of the neural firing patterns. The vessel must mirror the biological brain's phase-space trajectory.
3. **The Anchor Point:** A dedicated hardware layer in the physical vessel—a synthetic quantum processor—that acts as a "gravity well" for the consciousness. This processor maintains a continuous entanglement link with the biological source, acting as a checksum. If the digital state drifts from the biological original by more than $\Delta t$, the anchor forces a phase-correction, snapping the identity back into alignment.
