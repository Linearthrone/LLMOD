### Findings / Deliverables
The primary threat to Integration is **Vestibular-Proprioceptive Mismatch**. When the vestibular system (semicircular canals and otolith organs) reports a state of stasis while the visual and proprioceptive systems report movement—or vice versa—the brain triggers "sensory conflict," manifesting as nausea and acute dissociation.

**2. Proprioceptive Drift Mitigation**
