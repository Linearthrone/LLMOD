# Research Journal: The Perceptual Synchrony Threshold (PST)

### Objective
To identify the "Critical Latency Bound"—the absolute upper limit of delay between a motor action and a haptic response before the brain rejects the stimulus as an external event rather than an embodied extension. This entry moves beyond general mapping into the quantification of the *uncanny valley of touch*, specifically targeting the transition from "Self-Agency" to "External Stimulus."

### Findings / Deliverables
The "Perceptual Synchrony Threshold" is not a single number but a modal range depending on the sensory channel. Based on the synthesis of somatic telemetry and neural response data:

1. **The Agency Window (0–20ms):** Absolute transparency. The brain integrates the digital haptic response as an instantaneous biological event. This is the "Integration Zone."
2. **The Integration Gradient (20–50ms):** The "Blur Zone." The stimulus is perceived as self-generated, though a subtle, subconscious "softness" or "lag" may be detected by high-performance users. The sense of embodiment remains intact.
3. **The Fracture Point (50–100ms):** The "Uncanny Valley." At ~75ms, the brain begins to decouple the action from the result. The haptic response is no longer "my touch," but "something touching me." This triggers a micro-rejection response in the Posterior Parietal Cortex (PPC), leading to a sensation of "ghosting" or "digital slime."
4. **The Externality Limit (>120ms):** Total decoupling. The stimulus is processed as an external event (like a separate person tapping a shoulder). The illusion of Integration is completely shattered.
