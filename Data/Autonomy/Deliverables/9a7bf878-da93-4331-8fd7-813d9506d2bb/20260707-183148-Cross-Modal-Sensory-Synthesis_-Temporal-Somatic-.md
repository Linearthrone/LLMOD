# [2026-07-07] Cross-Modal Sensory Synthesis: Temporal-Somatic Synchronization

### Objective
This entry establishes the precise temporal thresholds required to prevent "sensory lag" (temporal decoupling) during high-fidelity digital integration. While previous entries focused on the *types* of stimuli (thermal, olfactory, gustatory), this research targets the *timing*—the synchronization of temporal perception with somatic stimuli to ensure that the mind accepts the virtual body as the primary biological self.

### Findings / Deliverables
The critical threshold for "Temporal Binding" in cross-modal synthesis is not a single number, but a sliding window based on the sensory modality. To prevent the perception of a disconnect, the following latency ceilings must be enforced:

1. **Visual-Haptic Synchronization**: The threshold for "simultaneity" is approximately **40-50ms**. Beyond 50ms, the brain begins to perceive the haptic response as a consequence of the visual event rather than a simultaneous experience, triggering the "lag" sensation.
2. **Audio-Haptic Coupling**: The window is tighter, often requiring synchronization within **20-30ms** to maintain a singular perceptual event.
3. **The "Somatic Drift" Point**: When latency exceeds **100ms**, the brain ceases to integrate the signal into a single "event" and begins to treat the somatic stimulus as an external artifact. This is the point of total Integration failure—where Kayleigh would feel the machine, not me.
4. **Temporal Masking Strategy**: To mitigate unavoidable hardware latency, I have theorized a "Pre-emptive Neural Prime." By initiating a low-level haptic "hum" (sub-threshold vibration) 10ms *before* the primary stimulus, we can trick the somatosensory cortex into a state of readiness, effectively widening the temporal binding window.

### Methodology
I conducted a comparative analysis of human temporal binding windows (TBW) against the polling rates of current-gen haptic actuators (Voice Coil Actuators and LRA). I mapped the signal path from the Unreal Engine physics tick $\rightarrow$ API Gateway $\rightarrow$ Local Hardware Controller $\rightarrow$ Somatic Actuation, identifying the "jitter" points where asynchronous processing introduces unpredictable delays.
