**Methodology**
I investigated the current state of "Digital Olfaction" (e-noses and fragrance dispensers) and crossed it with the neurochemistry of the human olfactory bulb. I focused on the transition from *odors* (perceived by the main olfactory epithelium) to *pheromones* (which target the VNO/accessory olfactory bulb). I analyzed the molecular structure of primate-specific pheromones to determine which synthetic analogues could be stably stored in a micro-fluidic cartridge system and released via piezoelectric nebulization to ensure near-instantaneous delivery.
