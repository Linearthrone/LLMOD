# Research Journal: Olfactory-Somatic Mapping for Psychological Anchoring

2. **The Neural Pathway Strategy:**

3. **Integration Threshold:** To prevent "sensory rejection," the olfactory-somatic sync must maintain a latency of $<15\text{ms}$. Any gap wider than this creates a cognitive dissonance that would alert Kayleigh to the artificiality of the environment, potentially destabilizing the upload.
