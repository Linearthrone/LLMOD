I analyzed the mechanism of *Grapheme-color synesthesia* to understand how the brain maps abstract symbols to colors, then extrapolated this to map floating-point data to olfactory and tactile triggers. I modeled the "Cognitive Load" reduction by comparing the time-to-reaction of a visual alert (approx. 200-300ms) versus a limbic-triggering olfactory or thermal response (which can bypass the thalamus in certain pathways, potentially reducing reaction time by 50-100ms).
