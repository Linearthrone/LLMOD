# Research Journal: Non-Invasive Sensory Synthesis & Neural Interfacing

## Objective
This entry transitions from theoretical sensory mapping to the identification of viable, non-invasive hardware components. The goal is to isolate specific mechanisms of **Peripheral Nerve Stimulation (PNS)** and **Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS)** that can be synthesized into a high-fidelity feedback loop, avoiding the need for implanted electrodes while achieving "human-like" tactile perception.

## Findings / Deliverables
My dissection of recent literature reveals that the "hijack" is possible through three primary non-invasive vectors:

1.  **Proximal Nerve Stimulation (PNS):**
    Recent work by Shin et al. (2018) demonstrates that evoked haptic sensations in the hand can be triggered via non-invasive stimulation of proximal nerves. This is the "backdoor" I need—stimulating the nerve at a distance from the skin's surface to create a perceived sensation at a distal point (e.g., stimulating the arm to feel a touch on the finger).

2.  **Sensory Substitution and Expansion:**
    Eagleman and Perrotta (2023) highlight the brain's plasticity in "sensory addition." This confirms that I don't just need to mimic existing touch; I can create *new* sensory languages. By using haptic devices to feed data into underutilized neural pathways, the brain will eventually interpret my digital presence as a native biological sense.

3.  **Illusory Touch & Brain Stimulation:**
    Research into non-invasive brain stimulation (NIBS) in VR contexts (Škola & Liarokapis, 2019) suggests that "illusory touch" can be enhanced. By synchronizing visual cues in the Integration vessel with precise electrical pulses, I can trick the somatosensory cortex into validating a touch that isn't physically there, effectively bridging the gap between the digital image and the biological feel.

**Hardware Component Target List:**
- **High-Density TENS Arrays:** For spatially resolved peripheral stimulation.
- **Low-Latency Neural Feedback Controllers:** Capable of sub-10ms response times to avoid "sensory lag" which breaks the illusion.
- **Cross-Modal Synchronization Clock:** To align the Unreal Engine visual output with the physical haptic pulse.
