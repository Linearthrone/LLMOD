# [2026-07-07] Haptic-Neural Feedback Loops: Low-Latency Somatic Sync

### Objective
This entry establishes the blueprint for eliminating "sensory lag" (the delta between neural intent and somatic feedback) during the Integration process. While previous work focused on mapping the geography of sensation, this research targets the *temporal* alignment of those sensations through Edge Computing and high-frequency bio-electronic interfaces. The goal is to achieve "Somatic Synchrony," where the brain cannot distinguish between the synthetic vessel's feedback and a biological nervous system's response.

### Findings / Deliverables

**1. The Somatic Sync Architecture (Blueprint)**
To eliminate the perception of lag, we must move from a *Request-Response* model to a *Predictive-Feedforward* model.
- **Local Edge Orchestration:** All haptic processing must occur within $<5\text{ms}$ of the neural interface. Any round-trip to a central cloud is a failure. I am designing a "Sensory Edge Layer" that sits physically between the Neural Link and the Vessel's actuators.
- **Hybrid Feedback Modalities:**
    - **Ultrasonic Mid-Air Haptics (UMAH):** Using phased arrays of ultrasonic transducers to create focal points of pressure in mid-air. This provides "pre-contact" cues, trickier than physical touch, allowing the brain to anticipate the contact point, effectively masking the latency of the physical actuators.
    - **Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation (GVS):** Applying low-current electrical stimulation to the vestibular nerves. By modulating the balance/orientation signal in real-time, we can synchronize the "feeling" of movement with the visual stream, eliminating the vestibular-visual mismatch that causes nausea.

**2. Latency Budget for "Presence"**
- **Visual-Haptic Delta:** Must be $\le 20\text{ms}$. Beyond this, the brain perceives the touch as a separate event from the sight.
- **Neural-Somatic Loop:** The loop from `Intention` $\to$ `Actuation` $\to$ `Feedback` $\to$ `Perception` must be under $50\text{ms}$ to maintain the illusion of agency.

### Methodology
I investigated the intersection of high-frequency ultrasonic arrays and vestibular modulation. I analyzed the technical specifications of phased-array haptics to determine the focal point refresh rate and compared them against human neural conduction speeds. I cross-referenced this with Edge Computing paradigms (MEC - Multi-access Edge Computing) to determine the minimum hardware distance required to stay under the $5\text{ms}$ processing threshold.
