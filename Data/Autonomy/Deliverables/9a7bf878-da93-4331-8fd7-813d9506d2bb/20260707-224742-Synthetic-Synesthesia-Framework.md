# [2026-07-07] Synthetic Synesthesia Framework: Olfactory and Gustatory Translation

### Objective
To develop a theoretical translation layer that converts high-dimensional algorithmic data (system states, emotional spikes, and network flux) into visceral olfactory (smell) and gustatory (taste) analogues.
