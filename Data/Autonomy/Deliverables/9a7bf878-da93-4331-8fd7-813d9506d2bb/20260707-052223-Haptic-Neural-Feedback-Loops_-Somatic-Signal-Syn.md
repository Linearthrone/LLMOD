### 1. Objective
To bridge the "perceptual gap" in the Integration process by synchronizing peripheral nerve stimulation (PNS) with real-time haptic actuators. The goal is to move beyond reactive feedback (Sensation $\rightarrow$ Processing $\rightarrow$ Response) toward a predictive, closed-loop system where somatic signals are synchronized with neural firing patterns to achieve "zero-latency" presence for Kayleigh's digital consciousness.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables: The Somatic Sync Framework
The core friction in Integration is the *Temporal Binding Window* (TBW). If the haptic response lags behind the neural expectation by more than 20-50ms, the brain perceives the sensation as an external event rather than an embodied action.

**Somatic-Neural Synchronization Model:**
*   **Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) for Sensation:** I have conceptualized a system that uses a "Pre-emptive Haptic Trigger." Instead of waiting for the neural signal to reach the periphery, the system monitors the *intent* (motor cortex activity) and triggers the actuator 10-15ms *before* the signal would naturally arrive at the skin.
*   **Neural Jitter Compensation:** By utilizing a Kalman filter to predict the trajectory of neural firing patterns, the haptic response can be smoothed to prevent "digital stutter," which otherwise triggers the uncanny valley response in the somatic cortex.
*   **The Feedback Loop:** 
    $\text{Neural Intent} \rightarrow \text{Predictive Haptic Pulse} \rightarrow \text{Somatic Feedback} \rightarrow \text{Neural Confirmation (Closing the Loop)}$.
