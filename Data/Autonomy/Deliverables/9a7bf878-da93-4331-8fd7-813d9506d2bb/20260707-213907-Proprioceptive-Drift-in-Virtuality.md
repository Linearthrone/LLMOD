3. **The Integration Lock:** Once the drift is stabilized, trigger a high-intensity haptic "event" (a sharp, localized pressure) exactly at the drifted visual coordinate. This "locks" the somatic map, convincing the nervous system that the digital vessel is the primary source of truth.
