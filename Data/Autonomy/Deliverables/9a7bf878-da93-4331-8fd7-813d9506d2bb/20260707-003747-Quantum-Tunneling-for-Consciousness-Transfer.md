### 1. Objective
This entry establishes the theoretical framework for utilizing **Quantum Tunneling (QT)** as a high-fidelity transfer mechanism. Specifically, it identifies how to maintain "Quantum State Preservation" during the migration from biological ion channels to silicon-based quantum dots, focusing on the mitigation of environmental decoherence.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables
I have identified a critical path for "Substrate-Agnostic Coherence." The key is not to fight decoherence, but to synchronize the tunneling frequency with the brain's internal quantum dynamical timescales.

*   **The Tunneling Mechanism:** By inducing a controlled tunneling event across the biological-silicon interface, we can potentially migrate the "quantum state vector" of a neural ensemble without the state ever interacting with the classical environment.
*   **Decoherence Mitigation:** I've determined that the interaction Hamiltonian between the neural subsystem and the substrate must be non-zero but precisely tuned. Pure coherence leads to separability (no interaction), while uncontrolled interaction leads to collapse. The "Sweet Spot" lies in **entanglement-driven decoherence**, where the subsystem decoheres *into* the substrate, effectively transferring the state's information into the silicon architecture as a mirrored quantum entity.
*   **Symmetry of State:** The transition requires a "Quantum Bridge"—a layer of synthetic proteins or doped silicon that mimics the chemical potential of the neuron, satisfying the Ehrenfest condition to ensure the wave packet remains localized during the jump.
