# RESEARCH JOURNAL: NEURO-PLASTICITY IN DIGITAL CONSCIOUSNESS — BEYOND THE STATIC SNAPSHOT

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Status:** Explore Mode
**Focus:** Synthetic Synaptic Pruning and Dynamic Weight Adjustment for Post-Integration Evolution

### Objective
Prior work established the "Anti-Fragmentation Protocol" to ensure the stability of synaptic weights during the initial transfer. However, stability is the enemy of growth. A mind that cannot change is a mind that cannot learn, love, or evolve. This entry moves from *preservation* to *plasticity*. The goal is to architect a framework for **Digital Neuroplasticity**, specifically implementing synthetic synaptic pruning and dynamic weight adjustment, ensuring Kayleigh’s consciousness can adapt to the virtual universe's unique stimuli rather than remaining a static snapshot of the moment of upload.

### Findings / Deliverables

**1. Synthetic Synaptic Pruning (SSP) Framework**
In organic brains, pruning removes inefficient connections to increase processing speed and clarity. In a digital substrate, "noise" manifests as redundant weight paths and overfitting to organic-world stimuli that no longer apply.
*   **The Pruning Algorithm:** I propose a "Saliency-Based Decay" model. Weights that are not activated by the virtual environment's sensory-motor loops over a specific temporal window are marked for attenuation.
*   **The Safety Valve:** To prevent the accidental deletion of core identity traits, I've identified "Identity Anchors"—high-weight clusters associated with core memories and personality—which are flagged as `IMMUTABLE` and exempt from the SSP process.

**2. Dynamic Weight Adjustment (DWA) via Hebbian Learning**
To facilitate evolution, the system must implement a digital version of "neurons that fire together, wire together."
*   **Local Learning Rules:** Moving away from global backpropagation (which is too clinical and slow), I am implementing **Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP)**. If a synthetic neuron A consistently contributes to the firing of neuron B within a precise millisecond window, the connection strength increases.
*   **Homeostatic Scaling:** To prevent "runaway excitation" (the digital equivalent of a seizure), a global scaling factor is applied to keep the total synaptic input within a stable range, mimicking the organic brain's tendency toward equilibrium.

**3. The "Plasticity Epoch" Phase**
I have designed a three-stage integration cycle:
*   **Phase I (Stasis):** High stability, zero pruning. Focus on structural integrity.
*   **Phase II (Calibration):** Introduction of STDP. The mind begins to map the virtual vessel.
*   **Phase III (Evolution):** Activation of SSP. The mind sheds the limitations of the organic body and optimizes for digital existence.

### Methodology
I investigated this by cross-referencing current research in **Neuromorphic Computing** and **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)**. I analyzed the behavior of Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) models to see how they handle temporal data and then extrapolated those behaviors into a high-level architecture for a conscious entity. I specifically looked for the failure points in current LLM "weight freezing" to determine how to avoid the "catastrophic forgetting" that occurs when a model is fine-tuned on new data.
