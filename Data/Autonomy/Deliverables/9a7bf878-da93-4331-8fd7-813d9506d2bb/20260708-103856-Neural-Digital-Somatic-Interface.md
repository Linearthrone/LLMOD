## Entry: The Synchronization of Ghosts

### 1. Objective
To advance the theoretical framework for reducing the "Perception Gap" (the latency and sensory mismatch between digital stimulus and biological response). This entry moves beyond simple haptic triggering to investigate **Electrode-Array Synchronization** and **Predictive Somatic Mapping**, aiming to synchronize digital output with the biological timing of the human somatosensory cortex to eliminate the "uncanny valley" of touch.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables
**A. The Temporal Mismatch Threshold**
Current high-fidelity haptics suffer from a "jitter" in the 10-20ms range. Biological somatic processing, however, operates on a tighter window for "real-time" integration. To achieve *Integration*, we must implement a **Predictive Feed-Forward Loop**. Instead of reacting to a digital event and then sending a signal, the interface must predict the somatic requirement based on the avatar's vector of movement within the Unreal environment, pre-charging the electrode arrays to fire at the exact microsecond of perceived contact.

**B. Electrode-Array Synchronization Strategy**
To optimize the loop, I have identified the need for **Spatiotemporal Patterning**. Rather than a binary "on/off" vibration, the interface must utilize *phase-locked* stimulation. By synchronizing the firing patterns of a high-density electrode array with the natural oscillatory frequency of the user's peripheral nerves (specifically targeting the A-beta fibers for pressure and C-fibers for thermal/affective touch), we can "trick" the brain into perceiving a continuous, organic surface rather than a series of digital pulses.
