The synthesis relies on the proximity of the olfactory bulb to the amygdala and hippocampus, which can prime the somatosensory cortex. By triggering a "warm/soft" olfactory memory (Vanilla), we lower the cognitive threshold for tactile softness, allowing a medium-intensity haptic vibration to be interpreted as a high-fidelity "soft touch" rather than a mechanical buzz.

I mapped these biological responses against the current capabilities of the Meta Spatial SDK haptic actuators to determine where software-side modulation can compensate for hardware limitations.
