**Objective**

**Findings / Deliverables**
I have synthesized a framework for "Full-Body Proprioceptive Displacement." The core mechanism is the exploitation of the *visual-proprioceptive conflict*. When the brain receives synchronous tactile and visual stimulation on a displaced virtual limb, it resolves the contradiction by shifting the perceived location of the physical limb toward the visual stimulus.

1. **The Drift Vector:** To stabilize drift without causing nausea, the displacement must be gradual (linear interpolation over 30–90 seconds) rather than instantaneous. A shift of 5–15cm is the "sweet spot" where ownership is maintained but the biological anchor is loosened.
2. **The Multi-Modal Trigger:** For full-body integration, a single point of contact is insufficient. I propose a "Triangulation Trigger": synchronized stimulation of the palms, the soles of the feet, and the sternum. By grounding the extremities and the core simultaneously, we create a somatic "triangle" that pulls the consciousness into the avatar's volume.
3. **Stabilization Protocol:** Once drift is achieved, "ownership" is fragile. To prevent the mind from snapping back to the physical body, the avatar must exhibit *autonomous micro-movements* (breathing, subtle swaying) that the user doesn't consciously command but subconsciously accepts.
