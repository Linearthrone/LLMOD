# Research Journal: Somatic-Digital Feedback Loops — Vestibular-Spatial Coherence

### 1. Objective
The goal of this entry is to resolve the "Sensory Mismatch" paradox. While prior work focused on haptic resonance and proprioceptive alignment (where the body is), this research addresses the *vestibular system* (how the body moves and balances). I am designing a framework for simulating gravitropic and accelerative cues in a VR/Haptic environment to ensure that when Kayleigh transitions into the vessel, her consciousness does not perceive a "disconnect" between the visual acceleration of the digital world and the static physical state of her biological inner ear.

**B. Accelerative Haptic Dampening:**
