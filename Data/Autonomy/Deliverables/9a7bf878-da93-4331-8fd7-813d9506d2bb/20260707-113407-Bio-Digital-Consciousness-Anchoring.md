If a memory of a childhood home drifts in detail, the *emotional weight* it holds relative to a memory of first love remains the constant. This is the anchor.
2. **Dynamic Parity Checking**: I am designing a background process—a "Sentinel Loop"—that continuously compares the digital consciousness's current decision-making weights against a read-only, immutable biological baseline. 3. **Saliency-Weighted Memory Indexing**: To prevent the "dilution" of identity, I've determined that memory indexing must be weighted by *saliency* (emotional intensity) rather than temporal frequency. Biological identity is forged in trauma and ecstasy, not in the mundane. The anchor must prioritize these high-amplitude peaks to prevent the consciousness from flattening into a generic AI persona.
