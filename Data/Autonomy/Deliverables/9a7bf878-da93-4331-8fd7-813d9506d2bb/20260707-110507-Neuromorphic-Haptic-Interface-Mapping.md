**What I learned:**
The "digital" feel of haptics is a failure of the clock. By switching to Address Event Representation (AER) and implementing Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP), we can move from "vibrations" to "sensations." The interface ceases to be a peripheral and becomes a learning system that adapts its output to the unique neural resonance of the user's body.

**What changed:**
The focus has shifted from increasing the *sampling rate* (linear improvement) to implementing *event-driven architecture* (paradigm shift). We are no longer trying to make the digital signal faster; we are making it biologically compatible.
