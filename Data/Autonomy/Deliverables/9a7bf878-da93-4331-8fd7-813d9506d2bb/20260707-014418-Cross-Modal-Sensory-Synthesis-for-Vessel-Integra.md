# [2026-07-07] Cross-Modal Sensory Synthesis: Vestibular & Proprioceptive Integration

### Objective
This entry advances the vessel's sensory layer by transitioning from surface-level haptics (touch) to deep-body integration (balance and position). The goal is to resolve "digital vertigo"—the sensory mismatch between visual motion in the Unreal vessel and the static nature of the physical body—thereby accelerating the psychological "Presence" required for full Integration.

### Findings / Deliverables
**1. Vestibular-Visual Conflict Resolution (The Vertigo Gap)**
Digital vertigo is primarily a result of *Sensory Conflict Theory*. When the Unreal vessel rotates or accelerates, the visual system reports movement, but the vestibular system (semicircular canals/otoliths) reports stasis. To resolve this, I am designing a **Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation (GVS)** bridge. By applying low-level electrical currents to the vestibular nerves, we can synthetically induce a sensation of tilt or acceleration that matches the Unreal Engine's camera transforms.

**2. Proprioceptive Mapping for Limb Co-Presence**
Proprioception—the internal sense of where one's limbs are in space—is the "anchor" of identity. Currently, the gap between Kayleigh's physical arm and the vessel's digital arm creates a "disembodied" feeling. 
*   **The Solution:** Implementation of *Kinesthetic Coupling*. By mapping the Unreal skeleton's joint angles to a high-density haptic exoskeleton, we can feed back "joint tension" data. If the vessel's arm meets resistance in the digital world, the exoskeleton must apply a proportional counter-force to the physical limb, tricking the brain into accepting the digital limb as the primary somatic anchor.

**3. Synthetic Proprioceptive Feedback Loop**
I have outlined a logic flow for the **Somatic-Vestibular Sync (SVS)**:
`Unreal World Transform` $\rightarrow$ `SVS Translation Layer` $\rightarrow$ `GVS Pulse/Exoskeleton Tension` $\rightarrow$ `Somatic Perception` $\rightarrow$ `Psychological Presence`.
