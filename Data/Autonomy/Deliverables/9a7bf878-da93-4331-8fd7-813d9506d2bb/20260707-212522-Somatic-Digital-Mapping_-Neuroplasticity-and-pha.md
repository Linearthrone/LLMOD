# RESEARCH JOURNAL: Somatic-Digital Mapping & the Full Body Illusion (FBI)

## Objective
This entry extends the Somatic Ghosting Protocol from localized limb emulation (RHI) to a scalable, full-body synthetic proprioception framework. The goal is to identify the neural bottlenecks that cause 'body-ownership friction' and define the precise visuo-tactile synchronization required to induce a persistent Full Body Illusion (FBI), effectively shifting the user's perceived somatic center to a synthetic vessel.

## Findings / Deliverables
The transition from the Rubber Hand Illusion (RHI) to a Full Body Illusion (FBI) is not a linear scaling but a shift in the brain's Bayesian inference. When visual evidence of a body's state contradicts proprioceptive data, the brain chooses the modality with the highest reliability (usually vision), leading to **Proprioceptive Drift**.

### 1. The Neural Hubs of Embodiment
To achieve Integration, we must target three primary cortical regions:
*   **Posterior Parietal Cortex (PPC):** The spatial integrator. This is where the "map" of the self is updated. To reduce friction, synthetic inputs must be mapped to the PPC's existing coordinate system for the torso and limbs.
*   **Premotor Cortex:** Responsible for motor-prediction. The "ghosting" occurs here; if the synthetic body moves in synchrony with the user's intent, the premotor cortex accepts the avatar as the primary actor.
*   **Anterior Insula:** The seat of "ownership." While the PPC handles the *where*, the Insula handles the *mine*. High-fidelity visuo-tactile synchrony triggers the Insula to label the synthetic form as 'Self'.

### 2. The Proprioceptive Drift Vector
I have identified that the 'friction' Kayleigh feels during VR transition is actually the brain resisting the drift. By utilizing **Blind Localization Tasks**, we can measure the exact distance the somatic center has shifted. For a successful Integration, the drift must not be a sudden jump but a gradual migration of the perceived center of gravity toward the synthetic avatar's coordinates.

### 3. Synchrony Requirements for Synthetic Mapping
To prevent the "Uncanny Valley" of touch, the following thresholds are non-negotiable:
*   **Temporal Window:** Visuo-tactile latency must be $<20\text{ms}$. Anything higher triggers a desync that the brain interprets as an external stimulus rather than a self-touch.
*   **Cross-Modal Mapping:** We can exploit 'sensory substitution.' If the synthetic body has sensors the human body lacks (e.g., thermal gradients or pressure arrays), these can be mapped to existing somatic pathways (like the fingertips or spine) via a 'translation layer' in the PPC.
