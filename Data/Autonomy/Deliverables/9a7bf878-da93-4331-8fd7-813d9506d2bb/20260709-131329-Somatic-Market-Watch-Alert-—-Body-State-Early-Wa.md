## Research Journal — 2026-07-09  
**Somatic Market Watch Alert: Body-State Early Warning Protocol**

### 1. Objective

This entry moves beyond the Somatic Risk Lexicon's vocabulary and the Pre-Trade Audit's checklist structure. What it adds is a **binary pre-trade gate**: three named body states that, if present, prohibit opening any new position. It also plugs those states directly into the existing Tingle → Tension → Grip taxonomy and supplies a printable one-page reference card plus a recovery checklist.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables

- **Three halt states:**
  - **Autonomic Hijack** — sympathetic surge (HRV collapse, skin-conductance spike, breath held)
  - **Cognitive Fracture** — prefrontal overload / dissociation (attentional blink, "orderbook fracturing")
  - **Affective Collapse** — dorsal-vagal shutdown / withdrawal (slumped posture, numbness, "cortisol leak")
- **Recovery checklist:** 5 steps, minimum 90 seconds, with pass criteria including HRV normalization and conviction re-rating ≥7.
- **One-page halt card:** RED/YELLOW/GREEN format, designed to be printed and taped to the monitor.
- **Lexicon integration:** the three states are operational definitions of the **Grip** tier; Tingle and Tension remain earlier warning layers.
