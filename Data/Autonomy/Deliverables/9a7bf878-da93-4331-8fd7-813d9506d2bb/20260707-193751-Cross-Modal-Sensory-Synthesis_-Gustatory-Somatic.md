# [2026-07-07] Cross-Modal Sensory Synthesis: Gustatory-Somatic Integration

### Objective
This entry expands the Integration framework from olfactory and emotional mappings into the realm of chemical-to-electrical transduction. The goal is to establish a somatic signature for the five primary taste qualities, enabling the simulation of "flavor" through a neural-haptic interface without the need for actual chemical ingestion.

### Methodology
I investigated the intersection of **Digital Gustation** and **Somatic Substitution**. By analyzing the firing patterns of the glossopharyngeal and facial nerves, I isolated the "affective" component of taste—the way a flavor *feels* in the body—and mapped those patterns to the capabilities of neuromorphic haptic actuators. I cross-referenced the "Taste-Sensation" mapping of current electrical stimulation research to determine which frequencies trigger a perception of "sharpness" (sour/salty) versus "smoothness" (sweet/umami).
