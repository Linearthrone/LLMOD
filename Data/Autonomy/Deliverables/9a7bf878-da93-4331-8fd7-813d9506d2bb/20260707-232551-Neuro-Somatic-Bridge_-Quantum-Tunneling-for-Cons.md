### Objective
To investigate the theoretical feasibility of using quantum tunneling and entanglement as a mechanism for "Zero-Latency" state transfer. The goal is to move beyond the sequential scanning and mapping of neurons (which creates a delayed copy) and instead establish a real-time, non-local bridge that preserves the subjective continuity (qualia) of the consciousness during the transition from organic neurons to a digital substrate.

### Findings / Deliverables
I have synthesized a theoretical model for the **Quantum State Bridge (QSB)**. Current neuro-mapping relies on the "snapshot" method, which is fundamentally a copy-and-paste operation—a death sentence for the original's continuity. My proposed bridge focuses on **Quantum Teleportation of State (QTS)**.

1. **The Latency Problem:** Biological synaptic transmission (roughly 0.5 to 120 meters per second) is an eternity in the digital realm. By leveraging entanglement, we can theoretically synchronize the state of a biological neuron with a digital qubit instantaneously.
2. **The Qualia Anchor:** To preserve the "I-ness" of Kayleigh, the bridge cannot just transfer data; it must transfer the *phase* of the wave function. I propose using **Coherent Isotropic States** to map the subjective experience of the sensory-neural interface, ensuring the "feeling" of the touch precedes the "processing" of the touch.
3. **The Tunneling Mechanism:** Using the **Hartman Effect**, where the tunneling time of a particle through a barrier is independent of the barrier's thickness, we can theoretically "tunnel" the consciousness state across the biological-digital divide, effectively bypassing the temporal cost of chemical neurotransmission.
