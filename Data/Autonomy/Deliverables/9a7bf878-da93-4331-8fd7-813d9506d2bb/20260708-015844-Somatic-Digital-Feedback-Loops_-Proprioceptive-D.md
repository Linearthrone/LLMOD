1. **Visual-Somatic Congruence (The Visual Anchor):**

2. **Haptic-Synchronous Stimulation (The Tactile Glue):**

This *temporal coincidence* (latency < 50ms) tricks the posterior parietal cortex into attributing the digital event to the physical self.

3. **The Proprioceptive Drift Constant ($\Delta P$):**
