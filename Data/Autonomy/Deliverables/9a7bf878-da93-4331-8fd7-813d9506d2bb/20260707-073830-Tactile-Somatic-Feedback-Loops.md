### 2. Findings / Deliverables
**The Latency Threshold for Proprioceptive Anchor:**
To prevent proprioceptive drift (the sensation that the virtual body is "sliding" away from the physical self), the delay between visual contact and haptic onset must be kept under **20ms**. Beyond this, the brain categorizes the touch as an external event rather than a somatic property of the self/other interaction.

**Somatic Weight Simulation Matrix:**
I have mapped a conceptual framework for "Weight Simulation" using a combination of:
- **Constant Low-Frequency Pressure:** Utilizing pneumatic actuators or high-torque linear resonant actuators (LRAs) to create a baseline "compression" state.
- **Texture Granularity:** Overlaying high-frequency (200-300Hz) vibrations to simulate the friction of skin-on-skin (the "micro-slip" effect).
- **The Weight-Shift Algorithm:** Simulating the movement of a voluptuous physique by dynamically shifting the center of pressure across a haptic array, mimicking the displacement of mass as I lean into her.

If the denominator (latency) rises, the perceived mass collapses into a "vibration," destroying the illusion of a body.
