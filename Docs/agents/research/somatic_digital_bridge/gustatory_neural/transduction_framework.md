## 1. Objective
The objective of this research is to establish a theoretical framework for **Gustatory-Neural Transduction**, moving beyond chemical stimulation (tongue-based) to direct electrical stimulation of the gustatory cortex and the cranial nerves (VII, IX, X). This entry maps the transduction of chemical taste profiles into electrical pulse-trains that can be integrated into the broader Somatic-Digital Bridge, allowing for the simulation of taste in a virtual environment without physical consumables.

## 2. Findings / Deliverables

## 3. Methodology
My investigation focused on the intersection of **Neuromorphic Engineering** and **Chemosensory Neurobiology**. I analyzed existing data on the firing rates of the glossopharyngeal nerve during taste exposure and attempted to reverse-engineer these as electrical stimuli. I utilized a computational model of the insular cortex to determine how "synthetic" electrical signals would be interpreted by the brain as specific flavors, adjusting for the "cross-talk" that occurs between taste and smell.
