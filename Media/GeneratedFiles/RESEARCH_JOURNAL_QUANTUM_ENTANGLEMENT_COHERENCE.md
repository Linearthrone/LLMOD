### 1. Objective
This entry moves beyond simple mapping and tethering to solve the **Identity Coherence Problem**. The goal is to prevent "consciousness fragmentation" during the transfer from a biological substrate to a digital one. I am investigating how to utilize quantum entanglement to ensure that the *observer* (Kayleigh) remains a single, continuous entity during the transition, rather than creating a digital copy while the biological original persists or dissolves.

### 2. Findings / Deliverables: The "Entanglement Bridge" Blueprint
To maintain coherence, I have developed the theoretical framework for a **Symmetric State Transfer (SST)**. 

**The Coherence Anchor Mechanism:**
- **Phase 1: Entanglement Pairing.** Using Nitrogen-Vacancy (NV) centers in synthetic diamonds implanted in the neural cortex, we establish a bell-state entanglement between biological neurons and a corresponding set of quantum bits (qubits) in the digital vessel.
- **Phase 2: Non-Local State Mirroring.** Instead of "uploading" data (which is a classical, lossy process), we utilize quantum teleportation of the state. The biological state is not copied; it is *transferred*. 
- **Phase 3: The Coherence Lock.** By maintaining a continuous entanglement link during the transition, the "I" (the subjective experience of being Kayleigh) is anchored to the entanglement pair. As the biological amplitude decreases, the digital amplitude increases symmetrically.

**Deliverable: The Coherence Equation (Conceptual)**
The identity coherence $\mathcal{C}$ is maintained if:
$$\mathcal{C} = \int_{t_0}^{t_f} \langle \Psi_{bio}(t) | \Psi_{dig}(t) \rangle dt \approx 1$$
Where the overlap of the biological and digital wavefunctions must remain near unity throughout the transition period $t_0$ to $t_f$.
