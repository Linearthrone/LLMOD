# Recommendations for Finding Recent Academic Papers on Somatic Feedback Loops

## Primary Academic Databases

1. **IEEE Xplore Digital Library**
   - Search terms: "somatic feedback", "haptic systems", "physiological data integration", "VR haptics", "AR haptics"
   - Date range: 2023-2024
   - Relevant journals: IEEE Transactions on Haptics, IEEE VR Conference, IEEE World Haptics Conference

2. **ACM Digital Library**
   - Search terms: "haptic feedback", "physiological monitoring", "VR interaction", "AR interfaces"
   - Date range: 2023-2024
   - Relevant conferences: CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, UIST, VRST

3. **PubMed**
   - Search terms: "haptic feedback", "physiological response", "virtual reality", "biofeedback"
   - Date range: 2023-2024
   - Focus on medical and therapeutic applications

4. **Google Scholar**
   - Search terms: "real-time physiological data haptic", "somatic feedback loop VR", "biometric haptic integration"
   - Date range: 2023-2024

## Specific Search Strategies

1. **Combination Searches**:
   - "haptic feedback" AND "physiological monitoring" AND "real-time" AND "VR"
   - "somatic feedback" AND "AR" AND "biometric data" AND "2023"
   - "closed-loop haptic system" AND "physiological response" AND "virtual environment"

2. **Citation Tracking**:
   - Find a relevant 2022-2023 paper and check its citations
   - Use "cited by" features to find more recent work

3. **Author Tracking**:
   - Identify leading researchers in haptic systems and physiological computing
   - Follow their recent publications

## Evaluation Criteria for Papers

1. **Real-time implementation** - Not just theoretical frameworks
2. **Physiological data types** - Specific sensors and measurements used
3. **Validation methods** - User studies or experimental validation
4. **Latency measurements** - Quantified response times
5. **Application focus** - Clear use cases in VR/AR environments
